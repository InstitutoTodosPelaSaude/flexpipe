### Why NC_003417.1?

The RefSeq complete genome for MAYV. Strain BeAr 20290 is widely used as the
reference in phylogenetic studies. Genotype L (lineage L) is the most common
circulating genotype in Brazil and South America.

## ViralQC — skip mode rationale

Mayaro virus is **absent from viralQC/datasets/blast.tsv** (the BLAST reference
set used for contamination detection). The dataset covers many sibling
alphaviruses — Chikungunya (NC_004162.2), Una (NC_043199.1), Madariaga
(NC_023812.1), VEEV (NC_075022.1), Ross River (NC_075016.1) — but **not**
Mayaro.

There is also **no Nextclade dataset** for Mayaro virus at the time of this
build (checked 2026-06-08).

Consequence: `viralqc.mode: run` would BLAST-mislabel every MAYV sequence as
its nearest alphavirus (likely Madariaga or Una, which are closely related
alphaviruses in the Semliki Forest antigenic complex); with a non-empty
`expected_virus`, this flags all sequences as `wrong_virus` → grade D → empty
build.

Strategy:
- `viralqc.mode: skip` — synthesize grade-A results for all sequences
- `genome_size: 11411` — skip-mode coverage = min(seq_len/11411, 1.0)
- `qc.min_coverage: 0.70` — filters sequences shorter than ~8 kb
- Contamination check is NOT available without a BLAST dataset; rely on NCBI
  fetch-time length filtering and taxid-scoped search (taxid 59301)

When a Nextclade dataset for Mayaro becomes available, switch to
`viralqc.mode: run` and set `expected_virus` to the appropriate alias key.

- 5' mask: 0–76 bp (annotated 5'UTR)
- 3' mask: 11145–11411 bp (266 bp past last CDS boundary)

**Review before production use**: the 3' mask is derived from the last CDS
endpoint, not an annotated 3'UTR. Biological review recommended.

The BED is active via `parameters.mask_sites_file`. Inline terminal masks are
set to 0 (disabled) to avoid double-masking.

## Corpus size (expected)

MAYV is geographically restricted to tropical South and Central America with
limited surveillance capacity. NCBI GenBank likely has on the order of 100–300
complete genomes (as of 2026). The build uses `min_sequences: 5` and
`sequences_per_group: 10` to accommodate a small corpus.

If the post-filter sequence count is below `min_sequences` (5), check:
1. Are sequences being length-filtered out? (`qc.min_coverage: 0.70` → ~8 kb min)
2. Are date fields valid? (INSDC sentinels are normalized at NCBI fetch)
3. Does any sample group have < 1 sequence? Lower `min_sequences` further.
